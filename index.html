About me

I’m a Data Analyst with a background in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science, passionate about transforming raw data into meaningful insights and solving real-world business problems.

I enjoy exploring data, identifying patterns, uncovering trends, and translating complex information into clear, actionable insights that support smarter decision-making.

My technical toolkit includes SQL, Python, Power BI, Excel, and BigQuery, with experience in data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, dashboard development, data visualization, and analytical problem-solving. I also explore Machine Learning and Generative AI to understand how emerging technologies can enhance data-driven solutions.

Beyond technical skills, I believe great analytics starts with asking the right questions, understanding business needs, and communicating findings effectively.

I continuously challenge myself through hands-on projects, practical problem-solving, and exploring emerging technologies. My goal is to bridge the gap between data, technology, and business decisions.

I don’t just want to analyze data. I want to discover the story behind it and turn that story into impact.
